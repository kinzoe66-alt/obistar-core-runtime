# Archetype Framework

## Purpose

Archetypes are:
cognition governance overlays.

They govern:
- pacing
- onboarding
- replay visibility
- trust signaling
- batching ergonomics
- interruption recovery
- clarification density
- progression visibility

---

## Important Constraint

Archetypes MAY alter:
- cognition presentation
- explanation density
- replay compression
- onboarding pacing
- batching behavior

Archetypes MUST NOT alter:
- operational legality
- authority legality
- continuity integrity
- replay integrity
- risk boundaries

---

## Core Archetype Types

### Guided Operator
Needs:
- reassurance
- visible progression
- onboarding depth
- replay explanation

### Exploratory Operator
Needs:
- rapid signal differentiation
- skimmable replay
- compressed lineage
- comparative prioritization

### Throughput Reviewer
Needs:
- batch review
- compressed evidence
- minimal friction
- rapid trust formation

### Teaching/Learning Archetype
Needs:
- cognition pacing
- reinforcement
- progressive abstraction
- confidence stabilization

---

## Future Direction

Archetypes become:
human cognition topology overlays
for operational environments.
